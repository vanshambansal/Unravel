# Unravel product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Website developers investigate visitor-reported browser bugs. Visitors record and submit without an Unravel account. This is a portfolio product with a bounded local MVP, not a commercial-scale SaaS launch.

## Product Purpose

Turn an explicitly recorded interaction into a private report, replay and correlated evidence, then developer-reviewed reproduction steps, an editable framework-independent JSON plan and standalone Playwright export.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The authoritative product requirements live in [PROJECT_BRIEF](../../docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md); actual implementation, checks and gaps live in [PROJECT_STATUS](../../docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md). Do not duplicate their capability inventory here. Preserve authentication, ownership authorization, immutable evidence, default masking, explicit recording and the isolated script-disabled replay boundary. A demonstration website is an integration target, not the product.

## Brand Commitments

Unravel retains its name, trace mark, charcoal/crimson identity and approved cinematic landing. The internal application is being redesigned: earlier frames are references, not the final quality bar. Current visual authority is [DESIGN](../../docs/DESIGN.md); the user selected B authentication and A product compositions, with coordinated material, continuity and evidence motion. Presence in Figma alone does not establish runtime acceptance.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing Figma file and native design assets, supplied internal references, current application code, synthetic fixture recordings, and local regression proof. Never invent users, metrics, root causes or passing tests. Use synthetic data in public design compositions.

## Product Principles

- Make the complete visitor-to-developer workflow understandable.
- Preserve privacy and expose unsupported behavior honestly.
- Require developers to confirm expected correctness.
- Make confident design choices; separate aesthetic review from functional testing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, readable text, responsive composition, reduced motion and recoverable states are required. Static Figma compositions cannot establish runtime accessibility or performance.
